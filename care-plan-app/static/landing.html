One minute.
Eight readings.

Each heartbeat changes the colour of your skin very slightly. Your camera picks that up, and our model reads it.

Heart rate

Beats per minute, from the colour of your face.

Breathing

Breaths per minute, from the rise and fall of your head and chest.

Breathing pattern

Whether your breaths are even, with pauses and deep sighs noted.

Heart-rate variability

How much the time between beats changes.

Pulse rhythm

How even the beats look in the video.

Pulse strength

To compare with your own earlier checks.

Alertness

Blinks and eye closure, the usual signs of tiredness.

Stress

From your heart rate, its variability and your breathing.
