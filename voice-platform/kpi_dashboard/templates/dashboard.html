Enterprise Infrastructure & Unit Cost Predictability

Every cent of operational cost is deterministic and directly tied to public API and hardware runtimes. With zero fixed per-agent licensing fees, costs scale strictly with usage: Groq LPU ($0.59–$0.79/1M tokens) handles multi-turn reasoning; Whisper Large v3 ($0.003/min) transcribes incoming audio; Murf Falcon-2 ($0.015/1k chars) synthesizes human-cadence voice streaming; and Carrier SIP trunking ($0.014/min) routes audio packets over Cloud Run. At $0.03575 per complete session, high-volume enterprise workloads achieve complete financial predictability.
