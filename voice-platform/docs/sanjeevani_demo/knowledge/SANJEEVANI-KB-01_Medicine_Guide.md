# Sanjeevani Medicine Guide

DEMO CONTENT for the recorded demo. A pharmacist must review and sign off every entry before real patients hear it. Doses below are the doses on the prescription; they are never calculated or changed by the agent.

## How to explain a medicine on a call
For each medicine say five things, one at a time, in plain words: its name, what it is for, how much, when and how often, and for how long. Add one caution if there is one. Then ask "Does that make sense?" Use the doctor's dose from the prescription record exactly. Do not read the strength in milligrams unless the patient asks.

## Crocin DS Syrup (paracetamol, 250 mg in every 5 ml)
What it is: a syrup that brings down fever and eases mild pain. On this prescription: 4 ml, twice a day, by mouth. Measure it with the dosing syringe or cup that comes with the bottle, never a kitchen spoon. Shake the bottle first. Caution: it contains paracetamol, so do not give any other medicine that contains paracetamol unless the doctor or pharmacist says so. Keep out of reach of children.
Plain line (English): "Crocin is the syrup for the fever and any aches. Give 4 millilitres in the morning and 4 in the evening, using the syringe from the bottle."
Plain line (Arabic): "كروسين شراب لخفض الحرارة وتخفيف الألم. أعطِ 4 مل صباحاً و4 مل مساءً باستخدام المحقنة المرفقة."

## Meftal-P Syrup (mefenamic acid 100 mg with paracetamol 125 mg, in every 5 ml)
What it is: a syrup for pain and fever. On this prescription: 4 ml, twice a day, by mouth. It is usually given with or after food or milk because mefenamic acid can upset an empty stomach. Caution: it also contains paracetamol, the same ingredient as Crocin, so the pharmacist looks at the two together. Do not add any other paracetamol medicine. Stop and tell the doctor if the child has stomach pain, vomiting, black stools, a rash or is not passing urine.
Plain line (English): "Meftal-P is the second syrup for pain and fever. Give 4 millilitres twice a day, after some food or milk."
Plain line (Arabic): "ميفتال-بي شراب ثانٍ للألم والحرارة. أعطِ 4 مل مرتين يومياً بعد تناول بعض الطعام أو الحليب."

## Giving syrups to a young child
Use the syringe or cup supplied, read the line at the dose, give slowly into the cheek, and write down the time of each dose on the temperature chart. Do not give a dose early because the fever is back; ask the doctor or pharmacist. Do not mix medicine into a full bottle of milk or food. Keep the bottles away from sunlight and out of reach. Discard opened bottles after the date on the label.

## If a dose is missed or a bottle runs out
Do not double the next dose. Say that the doctor or pharmacist decides what to do and offer to arrange a call. If the course is not finished and the bottle is empty, offer to arrange a refill through the pharmacy team.
