# Sanjeevani Doctors, Fees and Appointments

DEMO VALUES. The doctors, fees and hours below are made up for the recording. Replace them with Sanjeevani's real directory (edit backend/app/data/sanjeevani_care.json and run scripts/build_sanjeevani_kb.py) before any real patient call.

## How booking works
The booking tools are the only source of doctors, fees and free times. Find the right specialty from the symptoms, offer a senior consultant or a standard doctor, offer two free times, ask about insurance, say what the caller pays, read it back, and book. Never say a time or a reference number that a tool did not give. Payment is made at the visit. Ask the patient to arrive ten minutes early with their Emirates ID and insurance card.

## Senior consultant or standard doctor
A senior consultant has more years of experience and a higher fee. A standard doctor (a specialist, general practitioner or family physician) sees the same kinds of problems at a lower fee. Both are fully qualified. Let the caller choose.

## Insurance
Accepted insurers and the share of the fee they pay (demo values): Daman 80 percent, Thiqa 90 percent, GIG Gulf 80 percent, Sukoon 75 percent, Cigna 80 percent, NAS 70 percent. The patient pays the rest at the visit. An insurer that is not on this list is not guessed at: the full fee is payable. Insurance details are checked at the front desk on arrival.

## Cancelling or changing
A visit can be moved or cancelled up to two hours before the time. For a change, say the clinic team will confirm it, and do not promise a new time that the booking tool has not given.
