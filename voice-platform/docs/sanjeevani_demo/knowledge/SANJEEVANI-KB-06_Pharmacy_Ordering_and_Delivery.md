# Sanjeevani Pharmacy: Ordering and Delivery

DEMO VALUES. Prices, windows and rules below are made up for the recording. A pharmacist must approve them before any real order.

## What is needed to order
A valid prescription as proof (usually a PDF from the doctor), a photo of the patient's ID (Emirates ID or passport), a delivery address and a contact number. For now the prescription and the ID are sent through a one-time link; later this becomes a message link on the patient's phone. The agent never takes an ID number or card number over the phone.

## The steps, in order
1. Ask which medicines the caller wants, and start the order.
2. Send the prescription link. The link appears in the call transcript and is sent by message. The agent reads out a six-digit one-time code, slowly. The caller opens the link, types the code, and uploads the prescription (a PDF is best; a clear photo of the whole page also works) and a photo of their ID. The link works once and expires after twenty minutes.
3. Read the prescription back. The agent reads each medicine it found, with a short explanation, the dose only if the prescription was clear, and the price. The caller confirms each one.
4. Take the delivery address and a contact number, and read them back.
5. Send the payment link and read out the amount and the one-time code. The caller opens the link and confirms the payment.
6. Offer the delivery windows, schedule the one the caller chooses, and read out the order reference.

## Medicines the agent will not order
Controlled medicines and some prescription-only medicines need the original prescription and a pharmacist's approval. The agent does not place these orders: it says a pharmacist will call, and hands over to a person.

## When the prescription is not clear
If a dose, a strength or a name cannot be read, the agent does not guess and does not state a dose. It says the pharmacist will confirm that medicine before anything is sent.

## Delivery and price (demo values)
Delivery is from the next day, in two windows: 10 AM to 12 PM and 4 PM to 6 PM Gulf time. Delivery costs 15 dirhams, free for orders of 100 dirhams or more. Payment is made before the delivery is scheduled. Prices come only from the pharmacy list; a medicine not on the list is priced by the pharmacist.

## Substitutes
The agent never substitutes a medicine. If something is out of stock, the pharmacist calls to suggest an alternative approved by the doctor.

## Emergencies and doubts
If the caller describes an emergency, tell them to call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department. If they are unsure whether to take a medicine, or their symptoms are new or getting worse, suggest a meeting with a doctor and hand over to the Appointment Desk. The agent gives no medical advice.

## Refills
A refill needs the original prescription to still be valid. If the course is finished and the patient is still unwell, offer a doctor meeting first.
