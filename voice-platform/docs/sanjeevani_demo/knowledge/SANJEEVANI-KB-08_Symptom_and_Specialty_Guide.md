# Sanjeevani Symptom and Specialty Guide

DEMO CONTENT, for routing a caller to the right kind of doctor. It is not medical advice, and the agent never gives medical advice. The agent listens with sympathy, asks the related questions below one at a time, and then uses the doctor search.

## How to talk about it
Say one short sentence of real sympathy (for example, "I'm sorry you're not feeling well"). Do not say what the problem is, do not reassure that it is nothing, and do not suggest any medicine. Ask two or three of the questions below, one per turn, then search for a doctor.

## Emergencies come first
If any emergency sign below is mentioned, stop the booking: tell the caller to call 998 or go to the nearest emergency department now, and offer to stay on the line.

## General Medicine
What callers describe: fever, cold, flu, cough, sore throat, fatigue, tired, body ache, weakness, headache, stomach, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, diarrhoea, infection, diabetes, sugar, blood pressure, hypertension, checkup, check-up.
Ask about:
- how many days has it been going on
- whether there is a fever and how high
- any cough, sore throat or runny nose
- any vomiting, loose motions or stomach pain
- any other medical condition or regular medicine
Emergency signs (call 998): a fever above 39.5 C that will not come down, confusion, severe dehydration, or difficulty breathing.

## Pediatrics
What callers describe: child, my son, my daughter, my kid, baby, infant, toddler, newborn, vaccination, vaccine, growth, teething.
Ask about:
- the child's age
- how high the fever is and for how many days
- whether the child is eating and drinking
- any rash, vomiting or loose motions
- whether the child is unusually sleepy
Emergency signs (call 998): a baby under 3 months with a fever, a child who is very sleepy or hard to wake, difficulty breathing, a convulsion, or no urine for 8 hours.

## Cardiology
What callers describe: chest pain, chest tightness, palpitation, palpitations, heart, breathless, shortness of breath, irregular heartbeat, cholesterol, high bp.
Ask about:
- when the chest discomfort comes (rest, effort, after food)
- whether it spreads to the arm, jaw or back
- any sweating, dizziness or breathlessness
- any family history of heart problems
Emergency signs (call 998): chest pain with sweating, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, fainting, or severe breathlessness.

## Dermatology
What callers describe: rash, skin, itching, itch, acne, pimple, eczema, allergy, hair fall, hair loss, dandruff, psoriasis, fungal, hives.
Ask about:
- where on the body it is
- how long it has been there
- whether it itches, burns or spreads
- any new soap, medicine or food before it started
Emergency signs (call 998): a rash with fever, swelling of the lips or face, or blisters over a large area of skin.

## Orthopedics
What callers describe: back pain, knee, joint, bone, fracture, shoulder, neck pain, sprain, arthritis, ankle, hip, muscle pain, sports injury.
Ask about:
- where exactly the pain is
- whether there was a fall or injury
- whether there is swelling or difficulty walking
- how long it has lasted
Emergency signs (call 998): an obvious deformity, a bone showing through the skin, being unable to put weight on the leg after a fall, or numbness in the limb.

## ENT
What callers describe: ear, earache, hearing, nose, sinus, throat pain, painful throat, tonsil, tonsils, tonsillitis, swollen tonsils, difficulty swallowing, pain on swallowing, snoring, blocked nose, nosebleed, vertigo.
Ask about:
- which ear, nostril or side of the throat
- any discharge, fever or pain on swallowing
- any dizziness or hearing change
- how long it has lasted
Emergency signs (call 998): sudden loss of hearing, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or a heavy nosebleed that will not stop.

## Neurology
What callers describe: migraine, seizure, numbness, tingling, memory, dizziness, tremor, stroke, nerve.
Ask about:
- how often it happens and how long each time
- any vision change, nausea or sensitivity to light
- any weakness or numbness on one side
- any past head injury
Emergency signs (call 998): sudden weakness on one side, slurred speech, the worst headache of their life, or a seizure.

## Gastroenterology
What callers describe: acidity, gas, bloating, constipation, ulcer, liver, reflux, heartburn, indigestion, stomach pain, abdominal pain, jaundice.
Ask about:
- when the discomfort comes (before or after food)
- any vomiting, black stools or weight loss
- how long it has lasted
- any medicine already tried
Emergency signs (call 998): vomiting blood, black stools, severe constant stomach pain, or yellow eyes with fever.

## Ophthalmology
What callers describe: eye, eyes, eye pain, eyesight, vision, blurred vision, blurry vision, double vision, red eye, red eyes, itchy eyes, watery eyes, dry eyes, conjunctivitis, pink eye, stye, eyelid, cataract, squint, glasses, contact lens, floaters.
Ask about:
- which eye, or both
- whether the vision is affected (blurred, double, or any loss)
- any redness, discharge, swelling or sensitivity to light
- whether there was an injury, a splash or something in the eye
- whether the person wears glasses or contact lenses
Emergency signs (call 998): sudden loss or sharp drop in vision, a chemical splash in the eye, an injury or something stuck in the eye, or severe eye pain with a headache or nausea.

## Dentistry
What callers describe: tooth, teeth, toothache, gum, cavity, dental, root canal, braces, wisdom tooth.
Ask about:
- which tooth or side
- whether there is swelling or fever
- whether hot or cold makes it worse
- how long it has lasted
Emergency signs (call 998): swelling of the face that spreads toward the eye or neck, trouble swallowing or breathing, or bleeding that will not stop.
