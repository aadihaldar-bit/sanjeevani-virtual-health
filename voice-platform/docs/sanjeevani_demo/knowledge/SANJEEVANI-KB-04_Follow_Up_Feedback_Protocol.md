# Sanjeevani Follow-up and Feedback Call Protocol

DEMO CONTENT. How the second call (after the course ends) works.

## When the call happens
One feedback call per course, on the day after the last dose is due, inside calling hours. If several medicines end on the same day, they are covered in one call. For the demo the course is five days, so the call is on day six.

## Opening
Greet, say who is calling, confirm identity (see the Call Safety and Identity Policy), and say what the call is for: "The course of medicines for Aarav finished yesterday. I would like to hear how he has been. It takes about two minutes."

## Questions, in this order
1. Did Aarav take the medicines as prescribed: all of it, some of it, or none?
2. Has he recovered: fully, partly, or not at all?
3. Any side effects or anything that worried you? Record the words the parent uses.
4. Overall, how satisfied are you with how the medicine worked, from 1 to 5?

## If the answers are good
Recovered fully and satisfaction 4 or 5: thank them, remind them to finish anything still left, ask if they need a refill (record it, do not place the order unless the ordering step is active), close warmly.

## If the answers are not good
Partly recovered, not recovered, satisfaction 3 or lower, a side effect, or a worry: say you are sorry to hear it, do not give any advice, and offer a meeting with the doctor who prescribed it. Ask for two preferred days and times, read them back, and use the doctor meeting step. Create a ticket for the care team with the parent's own words.

## Urgent answers
Any symptom listed under Emergencies in the Call Safety and Identity Policy: stop the questions, give the emergency instruction, hand over to a person, and create an urgent ticket.

## What to record
Taken as prescribed (yes, partly, no), helped (yes, partly, no), satisfaction 1 to 5, side effects in words, doctor meeting wanted (yes or no), refill wanted (yes or no), and a one-line summary.

## Ticket priority
Emergency symptom: urgent. Not recovered or side effect: high. Satisfaction 3 or lower with no symptom: normal. Refill request only: low.

## Feedback after a doctor visit
There is a second kind of feedback call. When the clinic marks a visit with a doctor as done, the patient gets a call two days later. It asks, in this order: how did the visit go, did the medicine the doctor gave them work well, and how was their overall experience from 1 to 5 (and anything we could do better). A patient can also phone in to give this feedback, but only for a visit the clinic has marked completed. Someone who has never visited, or whose visit has not taken place yet, is told kindly that feedback opens after the visit, and is offered an appointment instead. Unhappy answers (a low score, medicine did not help, a worry) are met with an apology, no advice, and a follow-up meeting with the doctor arranged by the care team.
