# Sanjeevani Care Sheet: Child with a Fever

DEMO CONTENT for the recorded demo. This is the home-care handout the agent may read from when a parent asks. It is general guidance and does not replace the doctor's advice for this child.

## Keeping a temperature chart
Write down the time, the temperature, how it was measured (under the arm, in the ear or by mouth) and any medicine given with its dose. Check the temperature morning, afternoon, evening and whenever the child feels hotter or seems unwell. Bring the chart, or send a photo of it, to the doctor.

## Fluids and food
Offer small amounts of water, diluted juice or an oral rehydration drink often. Offer light meals when the child is hungry; do not force food. A child who is drinking, passing urine and playing a little between fevers is usually coping.

## Comfort at home
Light clothing, a comfortable room that is not too hot or too cold, and rest. Do not wrap a feverish child in blankets. Do not use cold baths, ice or alcohol rubs. Do not give aspirin to a child.

## Go to an emergency department now if the child
Has trouble breathing or breathes very fast, has a seizure, is very sleepy or hard to wake, has a stiff neck, has a rash that does not fade when pressed with a glass, has a blue or very pale colour, or cannot be comforted. Any fever in a baby under three months old also needs to be seen urgently. In the UAE the ambulance number is 998 (confirm with Sanjeevani before the recording).

## Call the doctor the same day if the child
Has a fever for more than three days, is refusing fluids, is vomiting repeatedly, has passed no urine for eight hours, has ear pain, a new cough with fast breathing, or is getting worse in any way.

## When the fever medicine is not enough
If the temperature stays high after the prescribed medicine, or comes back each time it wears off, do not give extra doses. Tell the doctor. The agent offers a doctor meeting and does not suggest a different dose.
